# Infinite Soft Tech – 30-Day Client Outreach Plan

Goal: 300 outreach contacts → 30–40 replies → 8–10 demos → 2–3 paying clients.
Rule: every day = **10 new WhatsApp/emails + 5 follow-ups + 1 LinkedIn post (Mon/Wed/Fri)**.
Track everything in your CRM (crmadmin) or admin → Leads. Reply to every lead within 24 hours.

## Where to find 10 contacts/day (all free)
- Google Maps: search "school in Ahmedabad", "manufacturer Naroda GIDC", "distributor Ahmedabad", "clinic Satellite" → call/WhatsApp number on listing
- Justdial / IndiaMART / Sulekha listings (phone numbers are public)
- LinkedIn: search "Owner / Director / Founder" + Ahmedabad → connect + message
- Industrial areas: Naroda, Odhav, Vatva, Changodar, Sanand GIDC (factories need ERP)
- Your own network: relatives/friends who own shops or factories – ask for ONE introduction each
