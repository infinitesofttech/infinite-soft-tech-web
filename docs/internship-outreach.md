Offer colleges a free 45-min session: "How to get your first IT job – skills + live project". At the end, share the internship link and take registrations on the spot. One session of 60 students → typically 5–10 sign-ups.

## 6. Make the internship convert
- Offer 2 free demo days before payment – lowers fear
- Give referral discount: bring 2 friends → 10% off each
- Show real project work + mentor names on the page
- Keep batch sizes small (10–15) and say "seats limited" only if true
- Let best interns work on client projects (this also gives YOU free capacity)
- Collect a Google review/testimonial from each batch
